We are delighted to present this prime elevated position in the heart of Mullaghmore with stunning sea and harbour views. Superb three bedroom detached home, beautifully presented throughout. Spacious accommodation with bright living areas and private rear patio.

Private parking, upgraded windows and oil-fired central heating.

Walking distance to the harbour, beach and village amenities.

This charming and spacious detached family residence is set on an elevated site in a prime residential position in the heart of Mullaghmore, taking full advantage of its position with magnificent sea views enjoyed from the front and rear of the property. The accommodation is laid out over two floors and comprises an entrance porch and hallway, a bright main living room with open fireplace, a superb kitchen/dining room with patio access, a utility room, cloakroom and shower room and a ground floor bedroom with walk-in wardrobe, together with a first floor landing, two further bedrooms (one with en suite) and an additional WC.

The property benefits from recently upgraded UPVC double-glazed windows and doors throughout and is heated by an oil-fired central heating system to wall mounted radiators. Outside, the property has a private driveway to the front and a parking area to the rear, together with a covered seating area and a generous, recently extended patio enjoying excellent views and a lovely private aspect. A right of way runs along the side of the house, providing access to a property to the rear.

Location

Killogue (Kilkilloge) occupies an exceptional residential position in the heart of Mullaghmore, one of County Sligoâ€™s most sought-after coastal villages. Elevated to enjoy wonderful sea views to both front and rear, the property is within easy walking distance of the harbour, beach and village amenities. Mullaghmore is a picturesque fishing village centred around its historic 19th-century harbour and Blue Flag sandy beach, and is a Signature Discovery Point on the Wild Atlantic Way. Renowned for surfing, swimming, sailing, fishing and coastal walks around Mullaghmore Head, the village enjoys spectacular views across Donegal Bay to Benbulben and the Dartry Mountains, with the iconic Classiebawn Castle providing a striking backdrop. Despite its tranquil coastal setting, Mullaghmore is well connected, with Sligo Town approximately 40 minutes away and Bundoran and County Donegal around 15 minutes, offering an ideal balance of scenic seaside living and everyday convenience.
